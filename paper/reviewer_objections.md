# Reviewer-objection audit

## 1. “You are redefining mechanism to mean identification.”
No. Mechanistic proximity, mechanistic modelling and mechanistic identification are related but distinct. The paper adds identification strength as an evidentiary dimension; it does not replace other meanings of mechanism.

## 2. “This is anti-genomics.”
No. Molecular and genomic measurements can be proximal, experimentally manipulable and highly identifying. The conditional claim is only that proximity alone does not guarantee discrimination among declared alternatives.

## 3. “The product non-identifiability is trivial.”
Concede the algebra. The contribution is the ecological evidence classification, the `k-1-r` dimension rule, the calibration-transport family, sharp joint set, breakdown factor and operational design/reporting consequences.

## 4. “The multiplicative model was chosen to manufacture the theorem.”
Pollination and seed dispersal independently use rate-by-effectiveness or quantity-by-quality products. The theorem audits an existing measurement architecture; it does not claim all ecology is multiplicative.

## 5. “Community service is a sum, not one product.”
Correct. `V_mE_m` is a within-type product. `sum_m V_mE_m` adds a separate across-type allocation ambiguity.

## 6. “The `k-1-r` rule assumes independent anchors.”
Yes. Redundant measurements do not reduce dimension twice. State `r independent direct channel anchors` every time.

## 7. “Channel and calibration anchors are being mixed.”
They solve different missing-information problems. Channel anchors reduce latent-stage equivalence dimension. Calibration anchors measure proxy conversion and cross-regime transport.

## 8. “The complete ecological chain is not necessarily multiplicative.”
Agreed. The theorem applies only after a positive multiplicative endpoint map is scientifically declared. Other maps require their own identifiability analysis.

## 9. “Relative proxy ratios cancel calibration.”
Only under transport stability, `q_1/q_0=1`. Absolute scale cancels; regime-specific conversion drift does not.

## 10. “Why believe Gamma?”
Do not infer it from the same W/X observations. Use external calibration, validation, instrument/design knowledge or a declared sensitivity grid. The breakdown factor reports the minimum distortion sufficient to overturn the conclusion.

## 11. “F and E intervals can be reported independently.”
No. The same `kappa` generates both ratios and every admissible pair satisfies `rho_F rho_E=rho_W`. The primary object is the joint set.

## 12. “The 34% claim depends on reference direction.”
Use `Gamma*=max(rho_hat,1/rho_hat)` or `eta*=|log rho_hat|` as the canonical robustness scale. “34% upward drift” is only a directional translation.

## Submission stop conditions

Do not submit if molecular and field evidence are intrinsically ranked; `orthogonal` is used to imply independence; `Gamma` is described as estimated from the same net/proxy data; N4 is described as a `delta->1` endpoint; channel and calibration anchors are conflated; `k-1-r` omits independence; sum-of-products is presented as one two-factor product; or elementary algebra itself is sold as the primary novelty.
