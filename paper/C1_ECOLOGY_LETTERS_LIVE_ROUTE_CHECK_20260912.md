## Verified current route

For a Perspective:

- a full manuscript is considered only after direct invitation or approval of an unsolicited proposal;
- unsolicited proposals should be **no more than 300 words**;
- proposals should describe the nature and novelty of the work and its contribution to the discipline;
- the publisher/editor guidance also asks authors to state the qualifications of the author(s) who would write the Perspective;
- proposals are sent by email to **both** `ecolets@cefe.cnrs.fr` and `ecolets2@cefe.cnrs.fr`;
- a Perspective is held to the **same novelty expectation as a Letter**;
- the journal explicitly discourages submissions that are principally a review, merely an opinion statement, or mainly a discussion of the authors' own work;
- successful Perspectives are expected to be broadly relevant to ecology and often use quantitative analysis to introduce the new perspective.
